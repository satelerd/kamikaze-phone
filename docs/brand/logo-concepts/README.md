# Kamikaze Phone Flip — AppIcon directions

Exploration only. These raster concepts are not production AppIcons and must
be redrawn as controlled vector geometry before shipping.

## 01 — Flip Trail

The phone is the board. A single orbit records the trick around it. This is
the clearest product story and the strongest starting point, but the generated
concept contains too much phone hardware and glow for a final small icon.

## 02 — Kinetic K

The ballistic path becomes a `K`. It owns the brand initial and can become a
compact monogram, but this draft is too literal and dimensional. A production
version should use one phone slab and one flat trajectory, not three devices.

## 03 — Catch Halo

Two interrupted arcs resolve around the caught phone. It is the calmest and
most game-system-like direction, but it risks reading as refresh/sync unless
the catch interruption becomes more distinctive.

## Selection gate

Before replacing `AppIcon`, redraw the selected direction and test it at
1024, 180, 120, 60, 40, 32 and 20 points in grayscale and in the current
near-black / volt / ion palette. The silhouette must remain recognizable
without glow, gradients, text or detailed camera hardware.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool from three separate
`logo-brand` briefs. Source prompts are recorded in the development handoff.
